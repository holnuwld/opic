### 1. 건강한음식 경험
**파일명:** `건강한음식 경험.m4a`

- **Question:**  
  Now, tell me about the last time you actually ate some healthy food. Where did you eat it and who did you eat it with? How did it taste and what good did it do for you? Give me all the details.

- **Answer:**  
  The last time I had a healthy meal was last month. My wife had an upset stomach that day, so she didn't want to eat anything too greasy or heavy. My family decided to order something light and healthy for her instead of cooking. We ordered vegetable porridge from a Korean porridge chain. It's a healthy dish made with rice, carrots, zucchini, and mushrooms, and it's easy to digest. Something people often eat when they're not feeling well.  
  That porridge tasted really clean and mild. It didn't have strong spices, but it was warm and comforting. The rice was cooked just right. After the meal, we felt much more relaxed and less bloated. My wife also enjoyed it, and we all agreed it was the right choice for that day. It was a simple meal, but it made us all feel warm and healthy.

- **한국어 번역:**  
  지난달에 건강한 식사를 한 적이 있습니다. 그날 아내가 배탈이 나서 너무 기름지거나 무거운 음식을 먹고 싶어 하지 않았습니다. 우리 가족은 요리하는 대신 아내를 위해 가볍고 건강한 음식을 주문하기로 했습니다. 한 한국 죽 전문점에서 야채죽을 주문했습니다. 쌀, 당근, 애호박, 버섯으로 만든 건강식으로 소화가 잘되며, 몸이 안 좋을 때 사람들이 자주 찾는 음식입니다.  
  그 죽은 맛이 정말 깔끔하고 순했습니다. 강한 향신료는 없었지만 따뜻하고 속을 편안하게 해 주었습니다. 밥알도 알맞게 잘 익었습니다. 식사 후 우리는 훨씬 편안해졌고 속 더부룩함도 덜했습니다. 아내도 맛있게 먹었고, 우리 모두 그날 최고의 선택이었다고 생각했습니다. 소박한 식사였지만 우리 모두를 따뜻하고 건강하게 만들어 준 식사였습니다.
